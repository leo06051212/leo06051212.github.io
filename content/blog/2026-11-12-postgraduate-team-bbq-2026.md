Bruce Sham and I recently got together with our PhD and master's students and visitors for a year-end team BBQ. It was a chance to take a break from research, share a meal, and spend time together outside our usual meetings.

Our postgraduate community brings together people at different stages of their research. Gatherings like this give us time to catch up beyond the projects and deadlines that usually fill our conversations.

Thank you to everyone who joined us, and to our students and visitors for their work and contributions throughout the year. It was lovely to celebrate together as 2026 draws to a close.
